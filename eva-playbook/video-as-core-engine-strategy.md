# Strategic Memo — Video as Core Engine (Thesis Validation)

## Verdict
**Validate the primitive. Challenge the scope. Do not pivot.**

"Capture → analyze → coach" is a real, defensible engine and it aligns with Eva's direction. But "video as our primary focus, across music + sport + marketing + storytelling" is a pivot away from your north star (the PE Fund) and is the opposite of focus when you're already stretched thin. Build the engine once; sequence the verticals. Don't resource four B2C verticals in parallel.

## Why "capture is the core" is half-right
- **Right:** multimodal capture (audio/video/emotion) is the input layer to a huge class of AI apps, and in an AI-saturated world authentic on-camera signal is itself the trust currency (your own LinkedIn playbook proves this).
- **Too broad to be a strategy:** capture alone is commoditized — everyone has a phone; Whisper/vision models are table stakes. The value is not in the capture. It's in the **closed loop**: capture → analyze → coach → re-capture, inside a specific domain. The moat is the domain feedback loop + distribution, not the recording.

## Alignment with what you're already shooting for
| Existing goal | How the video engine serves it | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **PE Fund (north star)** | Builds your authority → warms the raise. Eva-as-video-agency becomes a cash-flowing asset itself (build or buy). | ✅ Feeds the fund, doesn't replace it |
| **Storeys raise (This Week #1)** | The founder video + repurposed stack is the raise's marketing engine | ✅ Directly aligned |
| **Eva (AI agency/platform)** | Video DNA = capture→analyze→coach is a productized service line | ✅ This IS Eva's engine |
| **Authority & media (45%)** | On-camera presence = credibility signal for investors + brand | ✅ Already the playbook |
| **Micro apps for revenue (15%)** | Musician/athlete/viral apps are micro-apps | ⚠️ Aligned in form, but B2C — no current edge |
| **Acquisitions (buy, 20%)** | Video micro-apps could be *acquired* assets, not built | ✅ Build-vs-buy angle |

## The one beachhead to start with
**Executive & B2B video marketing** — not music, not sport. Because:
- You already have the thesis, the data (LinkedIn B2B report), and the target buyer (founders, operators, agencies).
- It feeds the raise directly (your own authority + investor warming).
- It's B2B (higher ACV, retainer, fewer customers to prove revenue) vs B2C musician/athlete (needs massive distribution you don't have).
- It's where the Video DNA engine you just specced already lives.

## Vertical sequencing (option value, not parallel resourcing)
1. **Now — Exec/B2B video marketing** (beachhead; feeds raise + Eva agency).
2. **Next — Viral-video analysis** (closest cousin to marketing; reuse the same engine on public video data; can power your own outreach + a sellable tool).
3. **Later — Storytelling enablement** ("tell your Storeys, easily") — this is the consumer/productized bridge and overlaps your brand; only after the engine + a customer base exist.
4. **Last / defer — Musician & athlete coaching.** Pure B2C, zero distribution edge, no domain expertise today. These are the shiny-object traps that kill a first-time fund manager. Note them, do not resource them.

## Build-vs-buy (ties to your acquisitions workstream)
If video micro-apps are the endgame, the PE Fund's job is partly to **acquire** cash-flowing video/SaaS businesses — not build every one from scratch. So "video micro apps" shouldn't all be built; some should be acquisition targets. That keeps the build pipeline focused (one engine) while the buy pipeline (acquisitions) captures verticals you'd otherwise have to build.

## Reuse your own doctrine (this is the strongest signal of alignment)
Your deal-scoring pattern — Mine → Match → Score → Package → Route — maps cleanly onto video:
- **Mine** captures (founder recordings, public viral clips, athlete/musician footage)
- **Score** video (clarity, virality, compliance, emotional resonance)
- **Package** insights (coaching notes, edits, repurposed assets)
- **Route** to the right output (coach, creator, investor, feed)

Same chassis, different fuel — which is the metaphor you already use. That's a good sign the thesis fits your existing mental model rather than requiring a reinvention.

## Operational recommendation
- **Do not pivot.** Keep the PE Fund as north star; video is an engine that feeds it.
- **Keep the Video DNA engine domain-neutral** (the spec I wrote already is) so any vertical can plug in later.
- **Resource only the beachhead now** (exec/B2B video marketing). Capture the other verticals as documented option value in the Eva playbook — sequenced, not parallel.
- **One line of credit, not four.** Musician + athlete ideas go in a "future verticals" backlog, not the sprint.

## Thesis to adopt (one sentence)
> Eva's capture→analyze→coach engine, beachhead in executive & B2B video marketing, feeding both the Storeys raise and a sellable agency asset — with music, sport, and storytelling as sequenced option value on the same engine, not parallel bets.
