# Eva Playbook — Video-First Authority (LinkedIn Business Influence, Jun 2025)

**Source:** LinkedIn "Business Influence" webinar (business.linkedin.com/advertise/webinars/25/06/business-influence) + 2025 LinkedIn B2B Marketing & Creator Intelligence Report. Webinar page is gated; thesis + stats reconstructed from LinkedIn, Hootsuite, Supergrow, Storykit, TopRank, Forrester coverage.

## Core thesis (one line)
LinkedIn is now video-first and creator-led; buyers form opinions in the feed from credible operators, not brand pages — and creator content now influences the final buying decision.

## The numbers that matter
- Video viewership +36% YoY; uploads +34–45%; short-form video creation growing 2x faster than all other formats.
- CEO / executive posts up 23–68%.
- Personal profiles generate ~8x more engagement than company pages; company-page reach down 60–66% since 2024.
- 56% of B2B buyers who use creator content rely on it at the final decision stage; 82% of marketers using creators call it essential for measurable ROI; 81% say pulling back = leaving revenue on the table.
- Always-on creator programs: marketers who skip always-on are 17x more likely to call their influencer program ineffective.
- LinkedIn Live: 7x more reactions, 24x more comments than pre-recorded.
- Educational video generates 3x more engagement than promotional; long-form (3+ min) outperforms short clips.
- Mobile-first: vertical, captions, hook in first 8 sec; video is shared 20x more than any other format.

## Actionable for our journey

### Authority & media workstream (today 45%)
- **On-camera video is now a trust signal.** In an AI-saturated feed, showing your face = credibility. This serves the signature-talk / personal-brand goal AND de-risks you for the raise (investors Google the founder).
- **Repurpose, don't create from scratch.** The Leadr signature talk + podcasts/keynotes you already have = a 4-tier stack: clips → direct-to-camera → quote cards → carousels. One recording = 30+ posts. Retrieval is the problem, not creation.
- **Post from the personal profile, not the Mangotec/Storeys pages** (8x reach). Cadence: 3–4 posts/week, ≥1 video per 4 posts, mix ~40% video / 30% image / 20% carousel.
- **Educational > promotional (3x engagement).** Teach deal scoring, DSCR, RCFE economics, "how I evaluate a cash-flowing business." Builds authority for the raise AND Eva's agency positioning.
- **Go live or long-form.** Live = 24x comments; long-form (3+ min) beats short clips. Use LinkedIn Live for deal-review office hours.
